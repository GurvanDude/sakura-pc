# Logos Sakura PC : prompts ChatGPT

Les logos actuels (`logo/*.svg`) sont des brouillons. Les vrais se font dans ChatGPT, **dans une seule
conversation**, dans l'ordre : d'abord la fleur de base, puis chaque variante « à partir de l'image
précédente », pour que toute la série garde la même fleur.

Format pour toutes : **carré 1024 × 1024, fond transparent (PNG)**. Une fois choisies, les images vont dans
`design/logo/` (`sakura-pc.png`, `launcher.png`, `light.png`, `optimizer.png`, `ai.png`).

Deux directions possibles : essaie la A, et si ça ne te plaît pas, la B.

---

## Direction A : verre et lumière (icône premium)

### 1. Sakura PC (la fleur de base)
> Premium app icon of a single cherry blossom (sakura) flower seen from the front: five rounded petals, each
> with the characteristic small notch at its tip, delicate stamens in the center. The petals look like
> polished translucent glass with soft pink gradients, from deep rose at the center to pale blush at the edges,
> with subtle inner glow, refraction and a thin bright rim light. Elegant, refined, high-end, like a flagship
> app icon of a luxury brand. Perfectly centered and symmetrical, generous margins, no text, no letters, no
> background scene. Transparent background. Not cartoon, not childish, no outlines, no faces.

### 2. Sakura Launcher
> Using exactly the same glass sakura flower as the previous image (same petals, colors and lighting), place
> at its center a small, minimal "play" triangle made of bright white light, slightly glowing, as if the flower
> launches something. Keep it elegant and premium. Transparent background, no text.

### 3. Sakura Light
> Using exactly the same sakura flower as before, turn it into a neon light sculpture at night: the petal edges
> become glowing neon tubes in vivid magenta pink (#ff0f9b), the stamens glow electric blue (#0a3cff), with a
> soft bloom of light around them, as in a Tokyo night street. The petals stay dark glass inside. Inside a
> rounded-square tile of very dark plum (#140818). Premium, cinematic, no text.

### 4. Sakura Optimizer
> Using exactly the same glass sakura flower as before, replace its center with a small, refined circular gauge
> (speedometer) in white and deep rose, its needle pointing high, made of the same glass material. Clean,
> technical and elegant. Transparent background, no text, no numbers.

### 5. Sakura AI
> Using exactly the same sakura flower as before, reinvent it as a "data" flower: petals made of fine glowing
> pink lines and tiny luminous nodes, like a circuit board or a neural network drawn in light, a bright white
> core at the center, a few particles drifting. Inside a rounded-square tile of very dark plum (#1a0b1f).
> Futuristic but delicate and beautiful, premium. No text.

### 1. Sakura PC
> Minimalist Japanese family crest (kamon) of a cherry blossom: five notched petals drawn with perfect geometric
> precision, flat, elegant, balanced negative space, in deep sakura pink (#e0236f) with a thin gold inner line.
> Looks like a high-end Japanese brand logo, timeless and refined. Centered, no text, transparent background.

### 2 à 5. Variantes
